ChitLan Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. About ChitLan

ChitLan is a community chat app for Bantayan Island, Philippines. Locals and visitors can join a public chat, send private messages, meet people in RandomChat and post to a community feed. This policy explains what information the ChitLan Android app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password), or, if you use Google sign-in, your Google name, email and profile photo.

Profile information you provide. Username, unique @tag, birthday, gender, short bio, interests, municipality, and an optional profile photo.

Content you create. Public chat messages, private messages, RandomChat messages, community feed posts and replies, reactions, and reports you send about other users.

Activity information. The last time you were active (used for the "online now" count), your daily streak, badges, message count, and the people you have blocked.

Location (optional). If you choose to verify that you are on Bantayan Island, the app asks for your device's approximate or precise location once, checks on your phone whether you are on the island, and saves only the result (a "Local" or "Visitor" status). Your coordinates are not stored. You can decline and still use the app.

We do not collect your contacts, microphone or camera data, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and show your profile in the app.
  • To deliver messages, feed posts and notifications.
  • To match you in RandomChat based on shared interests.
  • To show the Local badge, streaks and other badges.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, blocking and removing abusive accounts.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

4. What other people can see

  • Public chat and the community feed are visible to every signed-in member.
  • Your profile shows other members your username, photo, @tag, municipality, Local or Visitor status, streak, badges, bio and interests.
  • Private messages are visible only to you and the person you are talking to.
  • Your email address, birthday and gender are not shown to other members in the app.

6. Who we share information with

ChitLan runs on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and Google provides Google sign-in. These providers process data on our behalf to run the app and are governed by their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users. We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Children

ChitLan is for people aged 13 and older. The app asks for your birthday and does not let you set up a profile if you are under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using ChitLan, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your username, birthday, gender, bio, interests and municipality any time in the Profile tab.
  • Block people from their profile and report messages or posts that break the rules.
  • Location is optional, and you can withhold permission in your phone's settings.
  • You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, as provided by the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 and similar laws.

9. Delete your account and data

To delete your ChitLan account, email us at schorzen098@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my ChitLan account". We will delete your account, profile and the content you created within 30 days and confirm by email.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase using access rules that limit who can read and change it. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong password and sign out on shared devices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for major changes we will tell you in the app.
